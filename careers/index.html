Careers

Interested in working with us? We are an actively growing organisation, and we are frequently looking for capable and motivated staff to join our team. Most positions are remote, although some may be based in Prague or Phnom Penh.

Fundraising Intern

Location: Remote

Duration: 3 months

Weekly Hours: 10–20 hours

Application Deadline: Ongoing

Description & Duties:

  • Support fundraising and donor outreach activities
  • Conduct research on funding opportunities, donor priorities, eligibility criteria, and application deadlines
  • Support communication with partners
  • Assist with donor mapping and prospect identification for NGOs
  • Assist with preparing meeting materials, briefing notes, and background research
  • Contribute to the development and growth of fundraising activities

Requirements:

  • Basic knowledge of fundraising/non-profit sector
  • Research and analysis skills
  • Communication and writing skills
  • Interest in fundraising, international development & cooperation, or NGOs
  • Currently studying/recently graduated with an undergraduate degree in International Relations or related subjects
  • A reference letter from an employer or professor
  • Proficiency in English
  • Previous NGO/fundraising experience is an advantage but not required

Benefits:

  • Professional experience in international development
  • Multicultural work environment
  • Opportunity to learn research and writing skills
  • Valuable networking opportunities
  • Flexible hours

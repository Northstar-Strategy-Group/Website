Current Projects

Here you can find overviews of some of our ongoing projects. For more detailed information about a particular project, please get in touch with us.

Cambodia

Empowering Migrant Youth Through Agricultural Education & Entrepreneurship

Northstar conducts research and data analysis on Water, Sanitation, and Hygiene (WASH), optimising the methodological implementation of water distribution infrastructure and other WASH projects in Southeast Asia. We also provide risk management and monitoring & evaluation services to local networks and INGOs in the WASH sector.

Fundraising Initiatives

In partnership with Action for Development Cambodia (AFD) · Kampong Thom Province

We are working with Action for Development Cambodia to develop an initiative that equips migrant youths with the practical agricultural knowledge and business skills needed to pursue sustainable livelihoods.

By connecting agricultural education with entrepreneurial development, this project helps young people turn practical skills into viable economic opportunities and supports their long-term self-reliance.

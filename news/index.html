Northstar Attends EU Export Day in Prague

Northstar had the opportunity to attend EU Export Day in Prague, represented by Anna Anufrikova and Finn Chapman. This conference connected businesses, institutions, and professionals to exchange insights and explore international opportunities. It was a valuable opportunity to learn more about European Union trade and export opportunities, while connecting with like-minded professionals and organisations.

At Northstar, we believe that meaningful international partnerships and cross-border collaboration are essential to long-term sustainable development, so we were happy to take this opportunity to develop new partnerships and strengthen existing ones.

28th August 2026

Strengthening Czech-Cambodian Development Cooperation

Northstar were honoured to attend a development event hosted by the Embassy of the Czech Republic in Phnom Penh, represented by Ye Khaung Htet. The event brought together organisations throughout Cambodia to discuss opportunities for cooperation on development.

As a Czech-based development consulting firm, we are committed to building meaningful connections between Czech partners and local organisations to support impactful and sustainable development initiatives. We sincerely appreciate the Embassy of the Czech Republic in Phnom Penh for the invitation and for opening the door to further dialogue and potential cooperation. We look forward to creating meaningful new partnerships and deepening collaboration between Czech and Cambodian stakeholders.

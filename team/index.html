Our Leadership Team

Northstar’s leadership brings together backgrounds in international relations, policy analysis, medical science, fundraising, finance, and law. Despite this diversity, we are united by our direct experience in Europe and Southeast Asia. Several of our team members met as students of International Relations in the Czech Republic, and have since built careers across various sectors before re-uniting to form Northstar Strategy Group.

Anna Anufrikova

Chief Executive Officer

Anna is Northstar’s CEO, and oversees our critical operations in the European Union and Southeast Asia. Before joining Northstar, she worked in the medical field, an experience that shaped the meticulous, people-first approach she brings to running the company’s core systems and administrative functions.
